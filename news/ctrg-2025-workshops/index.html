Workshops at CTRG-2025: Hands-on Learning in Transportation Research

The 8th Conference of the Transportation Research Group of India (CTRG-2025), scheduled for 17–20 December 2025 at IIT Guwahati, Assam, India, features six workshops focused on advancing transportation engineering topics such as road safety analysis, pavement structural analysis, intelligent transportation systems, academic writing, traffic signal control, and machine learning applications.
