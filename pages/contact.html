How to Reach IIT Bombay

By Air

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (15 km). Taxi services and shuttles available.

By Train

Kanjurmarg Railway Station (Central Line, 2 km). Major terminals: Mumbai CST and Mumbai Central.

By Metro

Powai Metro Station on Line 7 (Andheri East - Dahisar East) provides direct connectivity.

By Road

Accessible via Eastern Express Highway and Powai-Vihar Lake Road. Ample parking available.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common inquiries

Admissions

Q: When do applications open for M.Tech/PhD programs? A: Applications typically open in February for the July session. Check the IIT Bombay admissions portal for exact dates.

Research

Q: How can I collaborate on research projects? A: Contact the relevant faculty member directly or email research@civil.iitb.ac.in with your proposal.

Industry Partnerships

Q: Does TSE offer consulting services? A: Yes, we provide consulting through faculty expertise. Contact kvkrao@iitb.ac.in for opportunities.

Events

Q: How can I participate in TPMDC conference? A: TPMDC is held biennially. Registration details are announced 6 months prior to the conference.

Facilities

Q: Can external researchers access lab facilities? A: Collaborative access may be possible through formal agreements. Contact the relevant faculty member.

Alumni

Q: How can I stay connected with TSE? A: Join our alumni network by contacting alumni@civil.iitb.ac.in and follow our social media channels.
